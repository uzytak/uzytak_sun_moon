## 1.0.0

- First public release under the MIT licence.
- `sunTimes`: sunrise, sunset and solar noon (NOAA solar equations), polar day and polar night.
- `moonTimes`: moonrise and moonset with topocentric parallax (Meeus low-precision lunar theory), days without a rise or set, Moon above or below the horizon the whole day.
- `moonIllumination`: illuminated fraction, phase and bright-limb angle.
- Checked against Swiss Ephemeris 2.10.03: 12 places (five of them above 64° latitude) x 24 dates in 2026-2028, see the accuracy table in the README.
